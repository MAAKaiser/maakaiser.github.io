Md Abdullah-Al Kaiser

I am a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering, currently at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, with a focus on analog and mixed-signal circuit design, in-memory and photonic computing, neuromorphic and in-pixel computing, and device-circuit-algorithm co-design. Previously, I completed my M.S. degree at the University of Southern California.

I earned my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology, graduating among the top students in my program. My internship experiences include technical roles at Apple Inc., where I worked on hardware-accurate MATLAB modeling and mixed-signal schematic design, and at Meta Reality Labs, contributing to advanced schematic and layout verification.
