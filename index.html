Md Abdullah-Al Kaiser

I am a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering, currently at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, with a focus on analog and mixed-signal circuit design, in-memory and photonic computing, neuromorphic and in-pixel computing, and device-circuit-algorithm co-design. Previously, I completed my M.S. degree (3.95/4.00) at the University of Southern California.

I earned my B.Sc. degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) with a GPA of 3.93/4.00, graduating among the top students in my program (ranked 8th out of 194). My industry experience includes internships at Apple Inc. and Meta Reality Labs. At Apple, I worked as a Hardware Tech Intern in the AMS team, focusing on mixed-signal circuit design, hardware-accurate MATLAB modeling, and verification. At Meta’s O&DR Lab, I contributed to the schematic and layout design of display pixels, along with physical verification tasks such as DRC, LVS, and PEX.
